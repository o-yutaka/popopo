## Description

Scripture Everywhere AI makes Scripture native to meaningful moments inside wearables, games, developer IDEs, social communities, and creator tools.

The judge story follows a runner reaching a physiological wall. The wearable first detects heart rate and effort. A local consent, crisis, privacy, and cooldown gate waits for a safe recovery window. Only after a quiet haptic cue and wrist raise does the Scripture card appear.

For eligible events, the backend uses Gloo OAuth2 client credentials and Completions V2 to return a bounded need, theme, tone, and safety decision. YouVersion Platform retrieves the canonical passage plus Bible attribution. Strict response provenance keeps demo mode separate from verified live sponsor calls.
